Acoustic work audit: interpretation and limits (2026-10-16)

Source evidence
pd_fsi.cpp VelocityDivergence* computes one u_div from inner and wall
velocity differences. AcousticDamperInnerforPD uses (u_div_i+u_div_j),
while AcousticDamperWithWallforPD uses 2*u_div_i. Individual powers of
these two forces have cross terms and need not each be non-positive.
PD_capsule_compression.cpp records:
  L_inner = -W_fluid_inner
  L_wall  = -W_fluid_wall - W_PD_wall
The rigid moving-plate acoustic reaction work is not separately included
in these legacy columns. The total raw acoustic transfer can therefore
be negative even when a prescribed boundary physically supplies energy.

Controlled counterexample, using the production wall-pair algebra
Take one fluid mass m=1, unit volume and density, scalar G=dW*e=1,
coefficient C=0.1, fluid velocity v=0, prescribed wall velocity w=-0.1,
and dt=0.01. Then:
  div = -2*(v-w)*G = -0.2
  F_fluid = 2*C*div*G = -0.04
  W_fluid = dt*F_fluid*(v+dt*F_fluid/(2*m)) = 8e-8
  W_boundary = dt*F_fluid*w = 4e-5
  legacy L_wall = -W_fluid = -8e-8
  dissipation = W_boundary-W_fluid = 3.992e-5 > 0
This disproves an unconditional sign test on the legacy wall column. It
is not a proof that every multi-particle explicit exchange is passive.

Correct assessment
With separately recorded boundary work, test the combined
  L_acoustic = L_inner + L_wall + W_boundary.
Without that measurement, retain raw signed work and negative-component
observations, and report the dissipation sign as unverified. Do not turn
that absence of measurement into a passivity claim. Moving a boundary
term from net transfer to supplied work and dissipation on both sides
leaves the total mechanical residual unchanged.

The existing trajectory, binary, physical parameters, CSV values and 5%
mechanical monitoring threshold are unchanged. A separately versioned
controller may review the sealed monitor's legacy warning while keeping
that monitor and its original output intact. Mechanical proxy closure,
force convergence and acoustic passivity are distinct validation claims.
The work identities remain necessary checks of bookkeeping, not sufficient
proof of physically correct energy storage, impermeability or fracture.
