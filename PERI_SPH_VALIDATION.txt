PERI-SPH original-size capsule validation workflow (2026-10-15)

Current scope
The original model is a 1 mm outer-diameter capsule with a 20 um shell,
three shell particle layers, a 0.58 mm plate stroke and 0.1 m/s plate speed.
The full physical duration is 5.8 ms. A completed smoke test does not validate
this full trajectory. Full-run results and refinement acceptance are stored
in the study directory, not inferred from a successful build or initialization.

Corrections motivated by the energy audit
The capsule now uses RigidBoxContactforPD for both finite translating plates.
Its normal force is the negative gradient of the recorded penalty potential,
including faces, edges and corners. The particle contact radius is dp/2,
k_i = ContactStiffness * V_i / dp^2, and U_i = k_i*penetration^2/2.
The same force gives the plate reaction and the boundary power. The contact
is evaluated at every PD position midpoint, rather than held fixed across
an entire fluid exchange. The old kernel contact remains available through
analytic_plate_contact=false; it has no validated potential-energy closure.

The default pd_pressure_acceleration_factor=0 removes acceleration pressure
extrapolation on deforming PD walls in both directions. Motion, actual
AverageVelocity and AverageAcceleration remain recorded. Non-PD rigid walls
retain the upstream acceleration extrapolation. Factor=1 restores the old
PD extrapolation for diagnosis. A constant-pressure interface unit test checks
net force balance and pressure-work/density-flux conjugacy at factor=0.
No material, density, viscosity, loading rate or physical size was changed.

Mass budget
mass_budget.csv records both particle inventories, per-particle mass changes,
shell/fluid/total mass, sum(rho*V)-sum(m), fluid volume, and liquid mass outside
the computational bounds. No escaping or disconnected particle is removed
from this inventory. A constant global particle mass is not proof that the
shell is impermeable. Dynamic shell leakage needs an independently defined
geometric enclosure and must not be inferred from this mass test alone.

Energy budget (all SI)
energy_budget.csv records per-step contact boundary work, fluid pressure and
viscous work on the upper plate, kinetic energy, gravity-potential change,
linear-EOS compression storage, shell stress-compliance storage proxy,
analytic contact penalty storage, total plastic work, physical viscous signed
loss, regularized contact-friction signed loss, PD/acoustic signed losses,
and hourglass signed transfer. Positive signed loss removes mechanical energy;
negative values are retained as evidence of energy creation, never clipped.
Acoustic wall loss includes the shell-side counterforce work.
The shell stress-compliance proxy is J*V0*(dev(sigma):dev(sigma)/(4G)
+ tr(sigma)^2/(18K)). Hughes-Winget hypoelasticity has no general exact finite-
strain potential here; the field is explicitly a proxy. Plastic work includes
hardening and rate dependence and is not labelled as heat. No calibrated
fracture energy has been implemented; changes from bond failure remain in the
unclosed mechanical residual. Hourglass work is measured rather than assigned
an invented storage potential. Riemann and splitting defects also remain in
that residual. Thus a small residual is useful evidence, not a thermodynamic
validation of the fracture model.

The separate kinetic-work identities use every actual velocity kick and its
midpoint velocity, including both additional acoustic kicks. These identities
must close to roundoff. They verify accounting and force units independently
of the mechanical storage proxy. A small kinetic identity error does not imply
a small global mechanical residual.

Original-size study
1. Build extra_3d_PD_capsule_compression and extra_3d_PD_framework_tests.
2. Run ctest --test-dir build -L peri_fast --output-on-failure.
3. Run python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p test_peri_study.py -v.
4. Create a new sealed study directory:
   python3 tools/peri_study.py plan --root build/peri_validation/STUDY_ID 
     --config tests/3d_examples/extra_3d_PD_capsule_compression/configs/full_capsule.cfg
     --executable build/tests/3d_examples/extra_3d_PD_capsule_compression/bin/extra_3d_PD_capsule_compression
5. Run its sequential queue:
   python3 tools/peri_study.py run --root build/peri_validation/STUDY_ID
     --memory-limit-gib 15 --baseline-memory-gib 13

The plan seals a complete source copy, executable, config hashes, Git HEAD and
patch. It starts the full baseline first, then halves both solid/acoustic CFL
at fixed particle geometry (dt_half, dt_quarter). It compares the full-stroke
reaction-curve relative L2 difference, peak reaction, total plate work and
final mean damage. A partial or failed run cannot be used as a reference.
The provisional screening tolerance is 5%, including the energy monitoring
level. This is an engineering gate, not a formal asymptotic error estimate.
The three/four/five-layer spatial groups hold the physical PD horizon at
20.1 um. Fluid and plate spacing refine with shell spacing. The spatial groups
therefore test particle quadrature at a fixed nonlocal length; the code does
not silently shrink the horizon and call that pure discretization convergence.
The dt_quarter three-layer case is reused as the spatial baseline.

Spatial runs require the time/energy gate to pass. A conservative memory screen
uses measured baseline RSS times refinement ratio^6 because fixed-horizon
neighborhood counts also grow with refinement. This is a screening upper
estimate, not a measured refined-case requirement. Move the sealed study to
a larger Linux node and profile refined initialization for an exact estimate.
A moved frozen executable requires compatible runtime libraries. If the target
node needs a rebuild, create a new study from the same source and rerun the
group; different executable hashes are never mixed in one convergence table.
The queue stops on numerical failure, a failed time/energy gate or the memory
screen. It never silently coarsens the geometry, increases loading speed,
relaxes density/Mach limits or starts concurrent large jobs.

Runtime and interruption
An original-size 2 us cost pilot used 203608 shell and 463051 fluid particles,
about 12.9 GiB peak RSS, and 100.73 s wall time before these final corrections.
An updated audit pilot completed 2 us with maximum density deviation 1.24e-8,
zero inventory mass error, maximum mechanical residual 3.67e-5 and kinetic
identity residual 4.70e-30 J. It used 13.0 GiB peak RSS and 134.42 s wall time
while smoke tests shared the CPU; the timed integration/output portion was
87.04 s. These are small-deformation data before plate contact.
The full trajectory is a many-hour to multi-day calculation on this workstation;
the 2 us pilot is not evidence of large-compression stability. Updated cost
pilots and logs remain in build/peri_validation. progress.csv is flushed about
every 30 seconds during accepted increments. Full fields and budget rows are
written at the configured output frames. PD bond/internal-variable restart is
not yet implemented: an interrupted simulation cannot resume from its VTP.
A completed case may be reused by the study queue; a partial one is rejected.
