# Bounded actual-PD normal qualification

This candidate uses 33 volume nodes (99 coordinates), all fixed intact directed
reference neighborhoods, local shape tensors and all 33 material histories. It
does not replace the body by a tetrahedron or a single averaged deformation.
The full true nonsymmetric material/global tangent enters the primal-dual
Newton equations. Physical mass remains rho0 times each fixed volume.

114 positive-area pole-panel surface samples provide 228 signed MLS normal
constraints. Their original signed gather and its exact transpose scatter are
the same map. A separate frozen 640-point surface map observes geometry and
does not change forces. The observation union includes the exact poles;
duplicated union areas are never treated as another physical integral.

The first prospective normal study uses a fixed stroke .01, duration .16,
16 accepted intervals of .01, and the same cubic smooth wall motion throughout.
A purely analytical preflight of endpoint-contact linear particle paths showed
that 8 intervals can hide 1.0259712548% of the total stroke as intermediate
penetration; 16 intervals reduce this geometric interpolation bound to
.2746921032%. This decision is made before native study freeze. It is not a
solver rerun, a changed wall speed, or evidence of physical convergence. The
native observer and independent reader minimize each cubic gap over the whole
interval, including derivative roots, at every frozen observation point. The
predeclared limit remains 1% of the total stroke, not 1% of the current stroke.

The JSONL observer saves every accepted state, all material paths and tangents,
the original global matrices and Newton residuals/directions, per-plate impulse
and interval reaction, actual-mass kinetic energy, material physical loss,
normal contact loss and algorithmic loss separately. Reactions are impulse over
actual interval dt, not invented instantaneous terminal forces. A rejected
candidate is written distinctly and never advances the accepted cursor.

Independent assessment reconstructs rational reference geometry, 95-digit
signed MLS and material path integrals, numerical endpoint tangents, all local
transpose assemblies, actual global matrices at every recorded Newton iterate,
KKT/linear residuals, accepted history and physical energy/work ledgers. It also
checks the exact rigidity rank and the equilibrium stiffness spectrum, global
frame covariance, free translation and explicit invalid-state rejection.
Prospective negative controls corrupt reference gradients, signed weights,
true Jacobian symmetry, kinetic-mass interpretation and dissipation labels.

The bounded campaign is normal only. Its numerical units and parameters are
declared benchmark inputs, not experimental metadata. Full S3 remains open:
three-dimensional tangential friction and a new complete transactional restart
with material/reference/neighbor/cache/contact/dual/algorithm/output cursors
must still be implemented and independently verified. S4 time/space/horizon,
